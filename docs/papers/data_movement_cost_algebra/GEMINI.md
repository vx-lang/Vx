# Instructions on writing technical documents like papers and presentations

## Papers

- The language should be formal, concise and precise.
- Use diagrams and tables to explain concepts and results.
- Provide a 'existing work' section that explains existing work in detail, and positions your work in context of that.
- Describe the concept in detail

## Presentations

- The language should be concise.
- Use diagrams and tables to explain concepts and results.
